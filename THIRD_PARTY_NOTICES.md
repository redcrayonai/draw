# Third-party notices

## Library logos

The canvas library's **AI logos** and **Cloud logos** (`client/src/node-logos.ts`,
generated by `scripts/vendor-logos.mjs`) draw other
companies' marks. Each one identifies that company's product in a diagram a
person or an agent draws. redcrayon is not affiliated with, sponsored by or
endorsed by any of them. The marks are not part of redcrayon's own branding, and
this repository's licence grants no rights in them. All marks belong to their
owners.

The artwork is used as published: never recoloured, cropped, rotated or
reshaped. Each brand's dark-background file shows on dark themes when it
publishes one. An owner who wants a mark removed can open an issue. Removing the id
from `module/src/node-icons.ts` stops new placements, and existing nodes keep
their label without the mark.
